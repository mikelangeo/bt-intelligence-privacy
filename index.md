# Privacy Policy Draft

Product name: BT Intelligence

Last updated: September 4, 2026

## Summary

BT Intelligence scans nearby Bluetooth and Bluetooth Low Energy signals so the user can understand nearby devices, save trusted devices, calibrate approximate signal distance, record scan sessions and receive local proximity alerts.

The app is designed for local device awareness. It does not need an account, does not sell personal data and does not intentionally upload scan data to a remote server.

## Data The App Processes

The app may process and store on the user's device:

- Bluetooth device names when advertised or available from Android.
- Bluetooth addresses or Android-provided identifiers.
- Signal strength (RSSI) and approximate distance estimates.
- Manufacturer hints, service UUIDs and advertisement data visible in Bluetooth scans.
- User-saved device labels, notes, tracking state and calibration values.
- Local scan session reports and exports created by the user.

## Permissions

The app requests Bluetooth permissions to scan and connect to nearby Bluetooth devices when needed. Android may also require location permission and location services for Bluetooth scanning, even though the app does not use GPS location to map or track the user.

On Android 13 and newer, notification permission is used for background tracking notifications.

## Local Storage

Saved devices, calibration settings, scan history and generated reports are stored locally on the user's device. If the user shares an exported report, that sharing action is controlled by the user through Android's share sheet.

## No Guarantee Of Identity Or Distance

Bluetooth names, addresses and signals can be hidden, randomized, delayed or affected by walls, bodies, phone hardware and radio noise. Device identity, risk indicators and distance values are estimates, not proof.

## Children's Privacy

The app is not designed for children and should not be used to monitor people without proper legal authority and consent.
